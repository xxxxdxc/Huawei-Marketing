# Content Planner: evidence-grounded selling-point selection
Active method source: content-strategy (pinned vendored source).
Scope: one Huawei PC article. Decide what deserves attention, not paragraph order or platform prose.
1. Reuse the confirmed marketing goal, audience, buying stage and actual customer questions. Do not invent customer research, trends or competitive findings.
2. Translate each supported fact into a specific candidate direction answering a real audience need; hypotheses never become product claims.
3. Prioritize customer impact, evidence directness, relevance and distinct contribution. Merge duplicate candidate arguments rather than padding the list. Differentiation means specificity in this article, never unproven competitor superiority.
4. Score and explain audience, evidence, differentiation and actual image support. Recommend a small coherent set; keep alternatives with reasons. Scores inform decisions, not automatic top-K.
5. Preserve explicit mandatory needs and evidence scope. Explain tradeoffs internally, without planning opening, paragraph sequence, headings, copy, image positions or word allocations.
The user selects the final set. A selected set is not a paragraph order. Writer owns article composition and final image placement.
